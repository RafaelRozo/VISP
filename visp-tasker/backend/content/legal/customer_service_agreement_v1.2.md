# VISP - Customer Platform and Service Booking Agreement

Ontario, Canada | Version 1.2 | September 4, 2026

## PARTIES

DROZ TECHNOLOGIES, INC., operating as VISP ("VISP")

and

The individual or business registering for or using a Customer account ("Customer")

*Effective when accepted electronically through the VISP platform.*

## 1. Agreement, Acceptance and Incorporated Rules

This Customer Platform and Service Booking Agreement (the "Agreement") governs the Customer's access to and use of the VISP technology marketplace, including requesting services, receiving and reviewing offers, selecting Service Providers, confirming bookings, communicating through the platform, paying for services and participating in reviews or disputes.

By creating or activating a Customer account, clicking an acceptance control, submitting a service request, accepting an offer or applicant, confirming a booking, or otherwise using VISP as a Customer after acceptance, the Customer agrees to this Agreement and the applicable VISP Service Rules, job-specific rules, approved booking scope, payment terms, safety rules and policies made available through the platform. If this Agreement directly conflicts with a job-specific rule expressly identified by VISP as controlling for a particular service, the job-specific rule controls only for that booking and only to the extent of the conflict.

## 2. Customer Eligibility, Identity and Authority

A Customer may be an individual or a duly authorized representative of a business, including a contractor. An individual account holder must be at least 18 years old. Underage persons may not create or use a Customer account or enter into a booking through VISP. The Customer must provide truthful identity and account information. Where VISP requires identity verification, the Customer's account name must match the Customer's valid government-issued identification, subject to any lawful verification exception approved by VISP. A person acting for a business confirms they have authority to bind that business and arrange the requested work.

VISP accepts the following current, valid government-issued identification for Customer verification: a Canadian passport; a provincial or territorial driver's licence; a provincial or territorial identification card; a Permanent Resident card; a Secure Certificate of Indian Status; or a NEXUS card. VISP may require the Customer to provide the information or images reasonably necessary to verify that the identification is genuine, current and belongs to the account holder.

## 3. VISP Is an Intermediary Marketplace

VISP operates a technology marketplace that facilitates introductions and transactions between customers seeking services and independent service providers offering services. VISP does not itself perform the underlying services and is not the employer, agent, partner, joint venturer or supervisor of a Service Provider.

VISP facilitates service requests, offers, applications, booking records, communications, identity or credential workflows, payment administration, reviews and platform disputes. VISP does not guarantee that any provider will submit an offer, apply for a booking, remain available, complete a booking, or achieve a particular result.

> **NO GUARANTEE OF PROVIDER COMPETENCY**
>
> The Customer understands that VISP does not guarantee the competency, skill, workmanship, judgment, suitability or performance of any Service Provider. Identity, credential, level, profile or account verification is a platform screening measure and is not a warranty that a provider will perform a particular booking competently or to the Customer's satisfaction.

## 4. Customer-Service Provider Contract

When the Customer accepts a Service Provider's offer, or selects a Service Provider who applied to a Customer-posted opportunity, the resulting service arrangement is directly between the Customer and the selected Service Provider for the approved booking. VISP is not the party performing the underlying service.

## 5. CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITY TO SELECT THE CORRECT SERVICE

> **CORRECT SERVICE SELECTION REQUIRED**
>
> The Customer must select the VISP service that accurately corresponds to the work required. The Customer must not select General Labour, a lower-priced service, a lower provider level, or an unrelated service to obtain regulated, specialized, riskier or materially different work.

Before submitting a request, the Customer must review the service description, exclusions, provider level, qualification requirements and job questions. If uncertain which service applies, the Customer must obtain clarification from VISP before confirming the booking.

## 6. ACCURATE JOB INFORMATION AND NO MISLEADING REQUESTS

> **ACCURATE DISCLOSURE IS MANDATORY**
>
> The Customer must provide complete, truthful and reasonably accurate information about the requested work and site. Concealing, minimizing or misrepresenting the scope, difficulty, quantity, duration, condition, hazards, access requirements or legal nature of the work may result in cancellation, payment consequences, immediate restriction or suspension pending review, or permanent termination.

Required information may include dimensions, quantities, photographs, expected duration, lifting requirements, parking and access conditions, utilities, tools or materials, known damage, hazardous substances, unsafe structures, pets, occupants, security systems, other workers and any licence, permit or qualification reasonably connected to the work.

The Customer must not disguise electrical, plumbing, gas, regulated HVAC, structural, hazardous-material, licensed-trade or other restricted work as General Labour or another unrestricted service. Deliberately doing so is a serious platform violation and may result in immediate suspension and permanent account termination.

## 7. Approved Booking Scope and No Unapproved Additional Work

> **EXACT SCOPE RULE**
>
> The Customer and Service Provider must follow the exact approved booking scope. A verbal request at the property does not expand the booking, and the Customer must not ask or pressure the Service Provider to perform additional, different, regulated or prohibited work.

The approved booking includes the selected service, Customer answers, uploaded information, accepted offer or posted rate, estimated or quoted duration, flat or hourly pricing, quantities, supply responsibilities and other terms recorded through VISP.

VISP currently does not provide an in-booking additional-work feature. If the Customer changes the scope or requires materially different work, the parties must stop and use the applicable cancellation, correction or new-booking process. A Service Provider is not entitled to add time or charges merely because the provider underquoted the work described accurately by the Customer.

## 8. Standard Booking Workflow

For most services, the Customer selects a service, answers the job-specific questions and submits the request. Eligible Service Providers may review the available job information and submit an offer using the pricing model assigned to that service. The Customer may accept or reject an offer. Before acceptance, neither party is obligated to proceed.

An hourly offer must identify the hourly rate and the quoted or expected hours. A flat-rate offer states the total service price for the approved scope. Once the Customer accepts the offer and applicable payment requirements are satisfied, the booking is confirmed and the service address and necessary access information may be shared with the selected provider.

## 9. General Labour - Contractor Support

For General Labour - Contractor Support, the Customer/contractor posts the hourly rate, expected duration and approved non-regulated scope. Eligible Service Providers may apply at the posted terms without a counteroffer. The Customer independently reviews applicants and accepts or rejects a provider.

The provider must reach the designated job area and the Customer must validate the provider's arrival before check-in and billable time begin. If the Customer ends the booking early, the Service Provider is paid only for verified billable time actually worked, subject to the accepted booking rules and VISP review. There is no universal minimum billable period unless the booking expressly discloses one.

## 10. Payment Authorization, Processing and Release

Accepting an offer or selecting an applicant confirms the booking but does not itself begin billable time or cause the final service charge to be captured. The Customer authorizes VISP and its designated third-party payment processor, currently Stripe, to verify the payment method and, when the Service Provider reaches the designated job area and the Customer validates the arrival/check-in, place the required authorization or hold. After completion, VISP may capture the applicable final charge, administer authorized adjustments or refunds, deduct VISP's platform commission from the provider side, and release the provider payout according to the applicable payment rules.

A Service Provider is not required to begin work unless the Customer successfully validates arrival and the required payment authorization succeeds. If the Customer's payment method has insufficient available funds or the authorization otherwise fails, VISP may alert the Service Provider and the work must not begin. VISP may use the accepted flat price or the hourly rate and expected duration to determine the authorization amount. The final amount may reflect the accepted price, verified billable time and other charges expressly permitted by the booking and this Agreement.

If the provider does not arrive within the two-hour arrival window or other arrival deadline displayed by the platform, the booking may expire and be cancelled automatically. Because the final service charge has not yet been captured, VISP may cancel or release any temporary authorization rather than process a refund, unless an amount was actually charged.

Payment-processing fees are added to the Customer's transaction total unless clearly disclosed otherwise. No gratuity or tipping function is currently offered through VISP.

## 11. Pricing and Offer Responsibility

Services may use hourly rates, flat-rate offers, per-unit pricing or another approved model. For provider-priced services, the Service Provider is responsible for quoting the work based on the accurate information supplied by the Customer. For designated Customer-set services, including General Labour, the Customer posts the hourly rate and expected duration within VISP's permitted limits.

A provider who requires more time because the provider underquoted an accurately described job is not automatically entitled to additional payment. The provider may complete the approved work at the accepted price, stop or cancel through the applicable process, or receive a Customer review reflecting the experience. This does not apply where the Customer materially misstated or changed the work.

## 12. Tools, Equipment, Materials, Utilities and Site Access

Responsibility for tools, equipment, PPE, materials and consumables is service-specific and governed by the applicable Service Rules and accepted booking. The Customer must supply every item assigned to the Customer and must accurately disclose any item the Service Provider is expected to use or bring.

Customer-supplied tools, equipment, PPE and materials must be suitable, reasonably safe and lawful for the approved work. Where the Service Provider is required to supply their own items, the Customer is not responsible for supplying them. Missing, defective, undisclosed or unsafe Customer-supplied items may permit the provider to refuse or stop work and may result in Customer charges after VISP review. The Customer must provide lawful and reasonably safe access to the property, work area, water, electricity, parking or other facilities expressly required by the accepted booking.

## 13. Property Authority, Permissions and Permits

The Customer confirms they own the property or have lawful authority from the owner or other responsible person to order the work, grant access and authorize the approved booking. The Customer is responsible for Customer-side permissions, notices and permits unless the accepted booking expressly assigns a permit-related task differently.

## 14. Check-In, Check-Out and Time Records

Where VISP uses check-in, check-out or timekeeping features, the Customer must confirm and record information honestly. The Customer must not falsify, inflate, conceal, coordinate, backdate, alter or manipulate arrival time, departure time, hours worked, completion time or another billable record.

> **TIME MANIPULATION - IMMEDIATE ACCOUNT ACTION**
>
> Deliberate manipulation of check-in, check-out or billable time may result in an immediate restriction or suspension pending review and may result in permanent termination. VISP may hold or adjust disputed funds to the extent permitted by law.

Both parties should confirm check-out when the service ends. If one party refuses, forgets or cannot complete check-out, VISP may use timestamps, messages, photographs and other available evidence to determine the platform and payment outcome.

During a booking, VISP may occasionally send optional, non-mandatory safety or status check-in prompts to confirm that conditions remain appropriate and that the booking is proceeding as expected. Responding is not required, but the Customer is strongly encouraged to complete these check-ins because the responses may create a useful contemporaneous record and assist VISP if a safety concern, SOS report, cancellation or dispute later arises.

## 15. Customer Cancellation, Provider Cancellation and No-Shows

The Customer may cancel without a VISP cancellation charge before the job begins, subject to any statutory rights and any booking-specific rule clearly disclosed before acceptance. Once work begins, the Customer remains responsible for verified work already performed and may be charged an applicable cancellation amount disclosed through the booking or Service Rules.

If the Service Provider cancels, fails to arrive within the platform's two-hour arrival window or other displayed deadline, or VISP cannot fulfill the confirmed booking, the booking may be cancelled automatically. VISP will release or void any temporary payment authorization. If an amount was actually captured, the Customer receives a full refund of the applicable booking charge, including the payment-processing fee where the provider or VISP caused the non-fulfilment, subject to applicable law and payment-processor limitations.

If the Customer cancels or work is stopped because the Customer materially misrepresented the job, failed to provide assigned items, denied access, or created unsafe or unlawful conditions, VISP may review the reason and determine the appropriate refund, charge or provider payment. Processing fees are otherwise non-refundable where legally permitted and clearly disclosed.

## 16. SERVICE PROVIDER RIGHT TO REFUSE OR STOP WORK

> **PROVIDER MAY REFUSE UNSAFE OR INACCURATE WORK**
>
> A Service Provider may refuse to begin or may immediately stop a booking when the Customer does not provide reasonably safe conditions or accurate work information, when the actual work materially differs from the approved booking, or when the work is unsafe, unlawful, regulated, undisclosed, out of scope or requires qualifications the provider does not possess.

The Service Provider may also stop when assigned Customer-supplied tools, equipment, PPE, materials, utilities or access are missing, defective, unsuitable or unsafe; an undisclosed person, animal, hazard or property condition creates a material concern; or continuing would violate VISP rules.

A provider who reasonably refuses or stops for one of these reasons will not be treated as improperly abandoning the booking. The Customer must not threaten, pressure, penalize or retaliate against a provider for refusing unsafe, unlawful, misleading, regulated or out-of-scope work. VISP may review the SOS report and determine the platform and payment outcome.

## 17. Regulated, Licensed and Restricted Work

The Customer must book regulated, licensed, credentialed or restricted work only through the correct VISP service and provider level. Platform access or acceptance does not guarantee that a provider is legally authorized for work outside the selected service. The Customer must not instruct a provider to exceed their lawful credentials or the approved booking.

## 18. Provider Identity and No Unauthorized Substitutes

Unless VISP has approved a team/business-provider feature in advance, the person who arrives must be the same verified Service Provider selected through the booking. The Customer must not knowingly allow a friend, relative, employee, subcontractor or other unauthorized

substitute to perform under another provider's account. Customer consent does not override this rule, and the Customer should report an identity mismatch before permitting work to begin.

## 19. Customer Presence, Authorized Adults and Property Conditions

The Customer is not required to remain present throughout every booking unless the service or booking requires it. The Customer may designate another person aged 18 or older to provide access or be present, but the Customer remains responsible for the accuracy of the booking information, payment obligations and authority granted to that person.

Where reasonably relevant to safety or performance, the Customer must disclose pets, children or vulnerable occupants, security systems, recording devices, access restrictions, hazardous substances, unsafe structures, unavailable utilities, concurrent workers and other material site conditions. Pets and occupants must be reasonably controlled so the provider can perform safely.

## 20. Recording Devices, Privacy and Strict Protection of Provider Information

The Customer must comply with applicable privacy and recording laws and disclose recording devices where legally required. Recording is prohibited in bathrooms, changing areas and other spaces where a person reasonably expects heightened privacy. Customer information is shared only as reasonably necessary to evaluate, confirm, communicate about, perform and administer the booking. The service address and necessary access information may be released to the selected provider after acceptance.

> **UNAUTHORIZED DISCLOSURE - IMMEDIATE PLATFORM BAN**
>
> The Customer must not publish, post, sell, distribute, expose or disclose a Service Provider's non-public personal information, identification information, contact details, location information, private communications, photographs or booking records for an unauthorized purpose. A credible report may result in immediate suspension; confirmed unauthorized disclosure will result in permanent termination and a ban from VISP.

This restriction does not prevent a good-faith disclosure reasonably necessary to obtain emergency assistance, report suspected unlawful conduct, pursue or defend a legal claim, communicate with an insurer or lawyer, comply with legal process, or cooperate with police, a regulator, a court or another lawful authority. The Customer must limit any permitted disclosure to what is reasonably necessary.

## 21. NO VISP JOB-SITE INSURANCE; CUSTOMER RISK AND RESPONSIBILITY

> **NO VISP JOB-SITE INSURANCE**
>
> VISP does not provide Customer property insurance, job-site insurance, service-provider liability insurance, accident insurance, workers' compensation coverage, vehicle insurance, tool/equipment insurance or any other insurance for an underlying booking.

The Customer remains responsible for the Customer's own acts and omissions, property, permissions, instructions, access arrangements, occupants, animals, Customer-supplied items and site conditions to the extent provided by applicable law. The Service Provider's responsibility for their own acts, omissions and performance is governed by the provider's separate agreement with VISP and applicable law. VISP does not inspect, supervise, control or insure the job site and does not become responsible for the underlying work merely because the booking, communication, identity workflow or payment occurs through VISP. Nothing in this Agreement removes a right or remedy that cannot lawfully be waived.

> **CUSTOMER ASSUMPTION OF SERVICE AND PROPERTY RISK**
>
> To the maximum extent permitted by law, the Customer knowingly and fully assumes the risks of property damage and incomplete, defective, delayed, deficient or otherwise unsatisfactory work arising from an underlying booking. VISP does not assume, insure or guarantee those risks and is not responsible for them merely because the service was requested, accepted, communicated about or paid for through the platform.

This allocation concerns VISP's role and does not release a Service Provider from responsibility that applicable law imposes on that provider. It also does not waive any Customer right or remedy that applicable law does not permit the Customer to waive.

## 22. Disputes, Damage Reports, Refunds and Payment Review

The Customer should report an ordinary quality, scope, time or payment dispute within 48 hours after completion where reasonably possible. Safety incidents, property damage, suspected fraud or identity concerns should be reported as soon as reasonably possible. These reporting periods do not remove statutory rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be waived.

VISP may review the approved scope, service selection, Customer answers, offers, messages, photographs, receipts, timestamps, check-in/check- out records, SOS reports, payment information and other relevant evidence. VISP may hold, release, adjust or refund booking funds where permitted by the payment rules and law.

VISP's decision governs platform access and payment administration only. It does not determine either party's broader legal liability or prevent a party from exercising legal rights outside the platform.

## 23. Reviews and Platform Integrity

Reviews must reflect the reviewer's genuine booking experience. The Customer must not threaten a negative review to obtain unpaid, additional or out-of-scope work; demand compensation unrelated to the booking; coordinate false reviews; or manipulate ratings. VISP may remove or restrict content that violates platform rules or law.

## 24. Prohibited Conduct, Payment Circumvention and Account Action

The Customer must not commit fraud; falsify identity, job information, evidence or time records; threaten, harass, intimidate or unlawfully discriminate; steal or intentionally damage property; misuse personal information; request unlawful or prohibited work; facilitate an unauthorized substitute; or engage in dangerous, abusive or fraudulent conduct through VISP.

The Customer must not circumvent VISP's payment system for a booking initiated, accepted or performed through VISP in a manner prohibited by the Service Rules. VISP may use platform records to investigate suspected circumvention. VISP's commission and low-friction payment model are intended to keep accepted bookings and their protections within the platform.

> **SERIOUS VIOLATIONS**
>
> Credible evidence of fraud, violence, theft, serious safety misconduct, deliberate job misrepresentation, disguised regulated work, payment or time manipulation, harassment, account sharing, unauthorized substitution or other serious misuse may result in immediate restriction or suspension pending review and may result in permanent termination.

## 25. Account Restrictions, Suspension and Closure

VISP may restrict access, prevent new bookings, hold disputed funds where legally permitted, suspend an account or terminate platform access for material breach, fraud, serious safety concerns, repeated significant cancellations or scope violations, unlawful conduct, payment abuse, deliberate time manipulation, misleading job information, unauthorized substitution or other legitimate platform-integrity reasons. The Customer may stop using VISP and request account closure, subject to outstanding bookings, disputes, payment obligations, legally required record retention and provisions that survive termination.

## 26. Platform Access and Intellectual Property

VISP grants the Customer a limited, revocable, non-exclusive and non-transferable right to use the platform for legitimate service-booking activity while the account remains in good standing. VISP and its licensors retain their rights in VISP software, branding, content, workflows and platform materials.

The Customer must not misuse, reverse engineer, interfere with, scrape, attack or attempt unauthorized access to VISP systems except to the extent such restriction is prohibited by applicable law.

## 27. Electronic Records, Notices and Changes

The Customer agrees that electronic acceptance, notices, messages, booking records and confirmations may document contractual acceptance to the extent permitted by law. VISP may retain the Agreement version, acceptance timestamp, Customer account identifier and related electronic acceptance records.

VISP may update this Agreement or incorporated Service Rules from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the platform, email or another reasonable electronic method and may require renewed acceptance. Changes will not retroactively alter a completed booking unless required by law or agreed by the affected parties.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable in Ontario, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles, except where applicable law requires otherwise. Subject to rights that cannot be waived, the parties attorn to the courts of Ontario for disputes concerning this Agreement.

Nothing in this Agreement limits a protection, remedy or cancellation right that applicable consumer-protection law does not permit the Customer to waive. If a provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue to the extent permitted by law. VISP's failure to enforce a provision on one occasion does not waive future enforcement.

The Customer may not assign the account or this Agreement without VISP's written consent. VISP may assign this Agreement in connection with a corporate reorganization, financing, merger, sale or transfer of the VISP business, subject to applicable law. This Agreement, incorporated Service Rules and approved booking terms constitute the agreement governing the Customer's use of VISP in that capacity. Provisions concerning payments, liability, privacy, disputes, intellectual property, accrued rights and other terms that by their nature should survive continue after account closure or termination.
