# VISP - Independent Service Provider Platform Agreement

Ontario, Canada | Final Legal Draft Version 1.3 | September 3, 2026

> **FINAL LEGAL DRAFT - COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED**
>
> This final legal draft is prepared for review and is not legal advice. It must be reviewed and approved by qualified Ontario counsel before production use or acceptance by Service Providers.

## PARTIES

DROZ TECHNOLOGIES INC., operating as VISP ("VISP")

and

The individual or business registering for a Service Provider account ("Service Provider")

*Effective when accepted electronically through the VISP platform.*

## 1. Agreement, Acceptance and Incorporated Rules

This Independent Service Provider Platform Agreement (the "Agreement") governs the Service Provider's access to and use of the VISP technology marketplace, including the ability to view, quote on, apply for, accept, perform and receive payment for service bookings made available through VISP.

By creating or activating a Service Provider account, clicking an acceptance button, submitting an offer, applying for a booking after being presented with this Agreement, or otherwise using VISP as a Service Provider after acceptance, the Service Provider agrees to be bound by this Agreement and the applicable VISP Service Rules, job-specific rules, approved booking scope, payment terms, safety rules and policies made available through the platform.

If there is a direct conflict between this Agreement and a job-specific rule expressly identified by VISP as controlling for a particular service, the job-specific rule controls only for that booking and only to the extent of the conflict.

## 2. VISP Is an Intermediary Marketplace - No Employment Relationship

VISP operates a technology marketplace that facilitates introductions and transactions between customers seeking services (each a "Customer") and independent service providers who choose to offer services through the platform. VISP does not itself perform the services offered by Service Providers.

The Service Provider uses VISP independently and is not an employee of VISP. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, worker, agency, partnership, joint venture, franchise, fiduciary or similar relationship between VISP and the Service Provider, except to the limited extent expressly required for VISP to facilitate platform functions and payments.

- VISP does not guarantee any minimum number of bookings, hours, earnings, customers or future opportunities.
- VISP does not require the Service Provider to remain available at any particular time or on any particular day.
- The Service Provider may provide services to other customers, businesses or platforms, subject only to lawful obligations the Service Provider independently accepts.
- The Service Provider is responsible for their own business decisions, taxes, expenses, legal compliance and service performance, except where this Agreement expressly states otherwise.

## 3. Customer-Service Provider Contract

When a Customer accepts a Service Provider's offer, or when a Service Provider applies for and is selected for a Customer-posted opportunity, the resulting service arrangement is directly between the Customer and the Service Provider for the approved booking.

VISP facilitates the marketplace interaction, records the approved scope, provides platform functions, processes payments through a third-party payment processor, applies the applicable VISP platform commission and may administer platform disputes. VISP is not the party performing the underlying service.

## 4. Voluntary Participation, No Assignment and No Guaranteed Work

The Service Provider is never required by VISP to apply for, quote on or accept any booking. The Service Provider may choose not to participate in any opportunity without owing VISP any obligation for declining it, subject to any separate obligations arising after the Service Provider has voluntarily accepted a confirmed booking.

VISP does not assign Service Providers to Customers. Where a Customer posts an opportunity, including General Labour - Contractor Support, eligible Service Providers may voluntarily apply and the Customer independently chooses whether to accept or reject an applicant.

## 5. Approved Booking Scope

Every booking is limited to the exact scope approved through VISP, including the applicable service description, Customer answers, uploaded information, agreed price or rate, quantities, duration where applicable, material terms and approved changes.

> **EXACT SCOPE RULE**
>
> For the protection of both parties, the Service Provider must follow the exact approved job scope; any work outside this scope is strictly prohibited unless formally added and accepted through VISP.

A Customer's verbal request at the job site does not, by itself, expand the approved VISP scope. Where VISP provides a scope-change function, the change must be recorded and accepted through the platform before the Service Provider performs the additional work.

## 6. Regulated, Licensed and Credentialed Work

The Service Provider must not offer, accept or perform work that legally requires a licence, certification, registration, professional designation, trade authorization, permit, clearance or other regulatory approval unless the Service Provider possesses all legally required authorization and is approved by VISP for that specific service where VISP requires such approval.

Access to one VISP level, classification or service does not authorize the Service Provider to perform another regulated or restricted service. The Service Provider must keep all credentials and authorizations submitted to VISP accurate, current and complete.

## 7. SERVICE PROVIDER RESPONSIBILITY FOR SAFE AND LAWFUL PERFORMANCE

> **CRITICAL PROVIDER RESPONSIBILITY**
>
> The Service Provider is responsible for determining whether they are capable, competent, properly qualified, legally permitted and appropriately equipped to perform each booking they voluntarily accept. The Service Provider is responsible for performing the approved work safely, competently and in accordance with applicable law.

- The Service Provider controls the manner in which the accepted service is performed, subject to the approved scope, applicable law, safety requirements and job-specific rules.
- The Service Provider must use reasonable care and must not undertake work they are not competent, qualified or legally permitted to perform.
- The Service Provider must use appropriate tools, equipment and personal protective equipment (PPE) where required for safe performance, subject to Section 11 where the approved booking permits Customer-supplied tools or equipment.
- The Service Provider must comply with applicable laws, site rules and legally required safety obligations.
- The Service Provider must immediately stop or refuse work that is prohibited under Section 14.

## 8. General Labour - Contractor Support

General Labour - Contractor Support is a VISP service for safe, non-regulated general labour assistance to contractors, businesses and job-site professionals. A Customer/contractor posts the hourly rate, expected duration and approved general scope within VISP's permitted limits. Eligible Service Providers independently choose whether to apply. VISP does not assign a Service Provider to the booking.

General Labour may include job-site cleanup, garbage and debris removal, loading and unloading, carrying and organizing materials or tools, moving supplies, grass cutting and basic grounds maintenance, basic assembly, production or assembly-line assistance, light non-structural demolition, site preparation and comparable non- regulated support tasks identified in the approved booking.

General Labour does not permit the Service Provider to perform electrical work, plumbing, gas work, regulated HVAC work, structural demolition, hazardous-material work, or any other work requiring a licence, regulated trade qualification, professional designation or governing-body authorization unless separately booked through an appropriate VISP service and lawfully performed by a properly authorized provider.

The Service Provider may use Customer/contractor-supplied tools or equipment where the approved booking permits it, but the Service Provider must not use equipment they are not legally permitted, competent or comfortable to use.

## 9. Platform Commission, Payment Processing and Payouts

VISP facilitates Customer payments through its designated third-party payment processor, currently Stripe. The Service Provider authorizes VISP and its payment processor to process booking payments, deduct the applicable VISP platform commission, administer authorized refunds or adjustments, and release the remaining payout to the Service Provider according to the applicable platform payment rules.

| VISP Provider Level | Platform Commission | Provider Share Before Other Authorized Adjustments |
|---|---|---|
| L0 | 17.5% | 82.5% |
| L1 | 17.5% | 82.5% |
| L2 | 15% | 85% |
| L3 | 10% | 90% |

The VISP commission is calculated on the approved service subtotal for the booking unless a job-specific pricing rule expressly states otherwise. The applicable commission may be displayed before the Service Provider submits an offer, applies for or accepts a booking.

Illustrative example: if an L0 or L1 Service Provider completes a booking with a $200.00 service subtotal, VISP's 17.5% commission is $35.00 and the Service Provider's payout is $165.00 before any other authorized adjustment.

Payment-processing fees charged in connection with the Customer transaction are, under VISP's current model, added to the Customer's transaction total and are not deducted from the Service Provider's payout, unless VISP clearly discloses a different treatment for a particular transaction before acceptance. Processor pricing may change and is disclosed through the applicable checkout or payment flow.

Payouts may be delayed, adjusted or temporarily held where reasonably necessary to address a payment failure, refund, chargeback, suspected fraud, platform dispute, safety investigation, inaccurate time record or other issue permitted by this Agreement or required by law.

## 10. Taxes, Registrations and Other Legal Obligations

The Service Provider is responsible for determining and fulfilling their own income tax, GST/HST, business registration, licensing, WSIB registration or clearance, insurance and other legal obligations arising from their independent activities, except to the extent VISP is legally required to collect, remit, report or withhold an amount.

The Service Provider must provide accurate tax and identity information reasonably required by VISP or its payment processor to administer payments or comply with law.

## 12. Pricing, Quotes and Customer-Set Opportunities

VISP services may use different approved pricing structures, including hourly rates, flat-rate quotes, per-unit pricing or other job-specific models.

For provider-priced services, the Service Provider may submit an offer only within the pricing parameters permitted by VISP for that service. Once accepted, the approved price is binding except where a permitted scope-change or job-mismatch process applies.

For designated customer-set services, including General Labour - Contractor Support, the Customer posts the hourly rate and expected duration within VISP's permitted limits. The Service Provider independently chooses whether to apply at the posted rate; there is no requirement to apply and no counteroffer unless VISP expressly enables one for that service.

## 13. Check-In, Check-Out and Hourly Time Records

Where VISP uses check-in/check-out or other timekeeping features, the Service Provider must record time honestly and accurately. Falsifying a check-in or check-out, coordinating false time records, remaining checked in when no service is being performed, backdating or altering hours, or otherwise manipulating a booking or time record is strictly prohibited.

For General Labour - Contractor Support, both parties may be required to confirm check-in before billable time begins. If the Customer ends the booking early, payment is based on the billable time the Service Provider was present and checked in, subject to the applicable booking rules and any dispute review. Where both parties confirm check-out, VISP may use the confirmed timestamps to calculate the final service amount.

> **TIME RECORD MANIPULATION - IMMEDIATE ACCOUNT ACTION**
>
> Any deliberate falsification, inflation, concealment, collusion or manipulation of check-in, check-out, hours worked, completion time or another billable-time record may result in immediate suspension and/or termination of the Service Provider account. VISP may hold a disputed payout while the matter is reviewed, to the extent permitted by law.

## 14. RIGHT TO REFUSE OR STOP UNSAFE, UNLAWFUL OR OUT-OF-SCOPE WORK

> **RIGHT TO STOP WORK**
>
> A Service Provider is never required to perform work that is unsafe, unlawful, materially different from the approved booking, outside the approved job scope, requires a licence or qualification the Service Provider does not possess, involves an undisclosed material hazard, or otherwise violates VISP rules.

The Service Provider may immediately decline or stop such work and may use VISP's SOS, cancellation, reporting or dispute tools where available. The Service Provider should identify the reason for stopping work as accurately as reasonably possible so VISP can review the platform record.

A Customer may not use the booking process to pressure a Service Provider into performing prohibited, regulated, unsafe or materially different work. VISP may review and take platform action when such conduct is reported.

## 15. SERVICE PROVIDER LIABILITY, DAMAGE AND INSURANCE

> **NO VISP JOB-SITE INSURANCE - RISK REMAINS WITH THE BOOKING PARTIES**
>
> VISP does not provide job-site insurance, service-provider liability insurance, Customer property insurance, accident insurance, workers' compensation coverage, tool/equipment insurance or any other insurance for an underlying booking. To the maximum extent permitted by law, the Service Provider assumes the risks arising from their own performance of the services. Nothing in this Agreement shifts to VISP any risk or obligation allocated to a Customer under the Customer's separate agreement with VISP or applicable law.

The Service Provider remains responsible for their own acts, omissions, negligence, misconduct, property damage, injury, claims, costs and service performance to the extent provided by applicable law.

Nothing in this Agreement transfers to VISP any risk or obligation that belongs to the Customer under the Customer's separate agreement with VISP or applicable law. This Agreement binds only VISP and the Service Provider and does not independently impose obligations on a Customer.

The Service Provider must maintain any insurance, WSIB coverage, registration, clearance or other protection legally required for the work and any additional coverage expressly required by the applicable VISP level or service. Customer responsibilities concerning insurance, permissions, property and site conditions are governed by the Customer's separate agreement with VISP and applicable law.

To the maximum extent permitted by law, VISP is not responsible for indirect, incidental, special, punitive or consequential losses arising from the acts or omissions of a Customer or Service Provider, except where such exclusion is prohibited by law.

## 17. PERSONAL PERFORMANCE AND ACCOUNT IDENTITY - NO SUBSTITUTE

PROVIDERS

> **NO SUBSTITUTE PROVIDERS - IMMEDIATE SUSPENSION**
>
> Unless VISP has expressly approved a separate team/business-provider feature for the booking in advance, the person who arrives and performs the booking must be the same verified individual whose VISP account/profile was selected by the Customer. Sending a friend, relative, employee, subcontractor, substitute or other person to "cover" a booking, or allowing another person to perform under the account, will result in immediate suspension of the Service Provider account pending review and may result in permanent termination. Customer consent does not override this rule.

Each Service Provider account is personal to the verified account holder unless VISP expressly designates the account as an approved business/team account with separately verified individuals. Account credentials must not be shared or transferred.

If more than one Service Provider is required for a booking, each person who performs work must have their own required VISP approval and must be separately identified or accepted through the platform where VISP requires it. An unapproved substitute may not perform any portion of the booking under another person's account.

VISP may hold any disputed payout associated with an identity/substitution violation while the matter is reviewed, to the extent permitted by law.

## 18. Account Restrictions, Suspension and Termination

VISP may restrict access to particular services, suspend an account, remove credentials or classifications, hold disputed payouts where legally permitted, or terminate platform access for material breach of this Agreement, fraud, serious safety concerns, credential problems, repeated significant scope violations, unlawful conduct, payment abuse, deliberate time manipulation, account sharing, unauthorized substitute-provider conduct, serious misconduct or other legitimate platform-integrity reasons. Where Section 17 requires immediate suspension for an identity/substitution violation, VISP will suspend the account pending review and may permanently terminate access after review.

The Service Provider may stop using VISP and request account closure, subject to completion of outstanding bookings, disputes, payment obligations, records VISP is legally required to retain, and any surviving provisions of this Agreement.

## 19. No Guaranteed Access to Services, Classifications or Customers

A VISP level, classification, credential review, account status or prior access to a service does not guarantee continued access to that service or create a right to receive any particular booking. VISP may apply job-specific eligibility requirements, credential requirements, service availability rules, geographic restrictions and platform- integrity controls.

The Service Provider must select or activate only the individual services they are willing and able to perform and must satisfy the applicable access requirements for those services.

## 20. Customer Disputes, Refunds, Chargebacks and VISP Review

Where a Customer or Service Provider raises a booking dispute, safety complaint, scope mismatch, payment issue or other platform concern, VISP may review information available through the platform, including the approved scope, messages, photographs, timestamps, check-in/check-out records, receipts, Customer answers, provider submissions and other relevant evidence.

VISP may make platform and payment-administration decisions reasonably necessary to administer the transaction, including releasing, holding, adjusting or refunding amounts where permitted by the applicable payment rules and law. VISP's platform decision does not prevent either party from exercising legal rights they may have outside the platform.

The Service Provider agrees to cooperate reasonably with legitimate platform reviews and to provide accurate information when requested.
